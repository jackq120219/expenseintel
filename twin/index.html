01 / Entry

What are you paying to get in?

Start with the price you know. ExpenseIntel fills a visible baseline around it; the adjustment lets you test a negotiated or alternative entry price.

02 / Carry

What keeps costing money?

Auto-filled values are planning baselines. Replace them with actual taxes, insurance, service, subscriptions, utilities or other known recurring costs whenever you have them.

03 / Stress

What else could the sticker price be hiding?

Twin can prefill a planning reserve for closing costs, change orders, repairs, migration, disruption or similar costs. Treat it as an envelope, not a prediction.

04 / Exit

What can you recover?

For assets with a plausible resale path, Twin can provide an illustrative recovery baseline. For costs that are not reliably recoverable, it leaves recovery at zero and says so.
