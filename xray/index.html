Drop in the number. See what it leaves out.

Paste a lease cost schedule, utility bill, contractor quote, operating budget, or listing text. X-Ray detects recurring and one-time costs, compares recognized lines with the location model, surfaces missing layers, and flags escalation language worth checking.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT: X-Ray starts with something people already have — a quote, bill, lease sheet or listing — then asks the more useful question: what does this number actually mean after hidden, recurring and location-driven costs are included?
