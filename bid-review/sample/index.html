Read this before choosing

B is $1,200 higher on the quoted bid, but only $400 higher once A's hypothetical $800 allowance is included. B's assumed annual operation/service cost is $170 lower, so its modeled total overtakes A after roughly 2.4 years.

What the model shows

B is $450 lower over five years under the invented figures. The calculation is reproducible but neither a verified savings claim nor evidence of contractor quality.

What remains unknown

Comparable equipment, electrical and permit scope, labor warranty, added fees and exclusions all require written clarification.

Action posture

Do not award on a $450 modeled difference until both bids have equivalent documented scope and substantiated cost assumptions.

04 / Move from numbers to an action.

Next action in this fictional case

Do not award a project on a modeled $450 difference. Obtain equivalent scopes and replace hypothetical cost assumptions with documented numbers before making the choice.

Prioritized requests
  1. 01
    Normalize scopeSend identical permit, electrical, removal and commissioning questions to both bidders.
  2. 02
    Confirm equipment and warrantyObtain model specifications, comparable performance ratings and complete labor/parts terms.
  3. 03
    Resolve budget exposureClarify exclusions, taxes, change-order rates, fees and payment milestones.
  4. 04
    Validate operating assumptionsRequest support for $25/$15 monthly operation and $150/$100 annual service figures.
  5. 05
    Update the decision recordRevise the cost table and retain written evidence supporting the final selection.

Ready-to-send clarification note

Before final pricing comparisons, please confirm in writing: permit and inspection responsibility, electrical and disposal scope, equipment model and capacity, parts/labor warranty, all exclusions and change-order rates, and operating-cost assumptions. Please identify potential additional charges.

Method and limits. Five-year net = bid + upfront allowance + 5 × (12 × monthly operating + annual service), less recovery when applicable. All case data are fictional. No contract, price, contractor credential, permit or utility bill was independently verified. This is decision support, not a contractor qualification or legal opinion.
