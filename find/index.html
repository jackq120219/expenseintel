Find / Location arbitrage
Do not wait for a broker shortlist to discover the economics.

Find the markets where your operation is mispriced in your favor.

Hold the operating profile constant, compare current public electricity evidence, project a bounded five-year burden, and surface markets where today’s cost and recent direction create an advantage worth investigating at the property level.

ARBITRAGE-SCAN BETA: Find uses current EIA state electricity pricing and building-use intensity evidence. It deliberately does not invent parcel taxes, labor, water, incentives or exact tariffs. The five-year lens caps recent price movement rather than treating one year of change as a guaranteed forecast.
01 / Operating fingerprint

Build the operation once.

The profile stays fixed while the geography changes. That lets ExpenseIntel ask a cleaner question: where does the same operation face a structurally different location-sensitive burden?

Electricity price and a bounded recent price trend change by market. Your operation and entered non-location cost stay fixed so the ranking does not hide assumption drift.
