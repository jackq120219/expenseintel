# ExpenseIntel Bid Review — $39 pilot go-to-market

Status: business offer draft. The $19 Deep Check is still sandbox-only and its webhook and secrets are not yet configured. Do not collect real $39 orders until a separate bid-review checkout, safe intake/fulfillment, and successful payment verification are operational.

## Promise and scope
- Price: proposed $39 per project (not $149). A human-reviewed report, delivered only when a real intake and fulfillment process has been established.
- The five-page PDF sample is fictional, not client work. Owner-ready output: executive briefing, cost decomposition with chart, scope comparison, assumption-based scenarios, and prioritized questions/action memo.
- Verify claims against actual supplied quotes; label all inferred exclusions as questions rather than fact. No contractor background check, professional advice, guaranteed savings, tax/legal assessment, or independent price verification unless actually performed.
- Avoid collecting confidential proposals on public forms. A secure private customer document workflow and deletion policy must precede collecting full contracts.

## Conversion landing page
- Direct 100 targeted B2B outreach emails to https://expenseintel.com/bid-review/ (only AFTER page is merged and publicly verified).
- Visitors can immediately inspect the fictional sample report and use the current free Check/X-Ray tools without replying or registering.
- Present the $39 service as coming soon/not yet accepting orders until production checkout and fulfillment are tested; no broken Buy button or live Stripe sandbox URL in marketing emails.

## Outreach compliance
- Use a real company sender address and validated business contact addresses, never guess addresses or scrape private mailboxes.
- Each outbound commercial email needs honest subject/sender, clear advertising identification, a valid sender postal address, and an easy opt-out; honor removals within 10 business days. Check applicable recipient jurisdictions and provider limits before sending.
- Send in small, personalized, verified batches. Maintain a suppression list and do not contact anyone who opted out. Avoid using BCC for a giant blast.
- Measure unique site visits, sample clicks, started free checks, inbound inquiries, opt-outs and paid conversions (only when real checkout is live). Do not claim success from send count.

## Launch gate
1. Publish and verify the public landing page and printable sample (same five fictional-page structure as reviewed PDF).
2. Establish a real business support/reply inbox and valid postal address, opt-out process and suppression list.
3. Build private secure intake, signed verified $39 checkout, owner matching, receipt and manual delivery SLA (confirm capacity and turnaround with operator).
4. Complete a test purchase and report fulfillment; only then switch to live Stripe, tax settings and terms.
5. Build verified prospect list of ~100 businesses with sources and relevant role; send individually or carefully batched emails after checks.
