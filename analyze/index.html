Analyze / Property diligence
The address is only the beginning.

Turn one location into a decision case.

Analyze combines the cost model with diligence: how resilient the commitment is, what financial obligations are still unresolved, what can shock the economics, what evidence you have, and what would need to change in the deal for the location to work.
