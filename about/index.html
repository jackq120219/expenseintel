About / ExpenseIntel

Decision intelligence for the physical world.

ExpenseIntel is a physical project and location decision-intelligence platform built by Queenan Capital. It helps builders, buyers, operators and property decision-makers test a place and a plan together before money, design or approvals become difficult to reverse.

Identity / Disambiguation

ExpenseIntel is about projects and places — not employee expense management.

The product is not a corporate-card, bookkeeping, reimbursement, or employee-spend platform. ExpenseIntel focuses on physical projects, locations, real-world cost layers, constraints, failure patterns and decision risk.
