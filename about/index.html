About / ExpenseIntel

Intelligence before you spend.

ExpenseIntel is a pre-commitment cost and decision-intelligence platform built by Queenan Capital. It is designed for meaningful consumer and business decisions where the sticker price alone is not enough: purchases, quotes, repairs, moves, leases, equipment, locations, renovations and physical projects.

Identity / Disambiguation

ExpenseIntel is decision intelligence — not expense management.

The product is not a corporate-card, employee reimbursement, bookkeeping, accounting or spend-approval platform. ExpenseIntel works before or around a meaningful commitment: understand the economics, expose what is missing, and decide what deserves verification.

CONSUMERBuy → understand

Cars, homes, repairs, moves, appliances, services and other meaningful purchases.

BUSINESSSpend → compare

Equipment, vendors, leases, facilities, locations and operating commitments.

PROJECTPlan → stress-test

Utilities, approvals, dependencies, hard-to-reverse work and failure patterns.
