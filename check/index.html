What the check is doing

Identify the decision

Read the public listing metadata or the text you pasted, capture the category and price, and keep the original source visible.

02

Pull category-specific public evidence: BLS, Treasury, EIA, Freddie Mac, FHFA, NHTSA, EPA/DOE and the deeper ExpenseIntel data layers where relevant.

03

Expose the gap

Show the facts ExpenseIntel still cannot verify: transaction comps, insurance, taxes, depreciation, exact scope or other decision-specific unknowns.
