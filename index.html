05 / Data philosophy

Explain the estimate. Never hide weak data behind a confidence score.

ExpenseIntel should combine authoritative public data, local schedules, property characteristics and transparent modeling. When a cost is uncertain, the product should show a range and the assumption behind it rather than manufacture precision.

Utility tariffsProvider and rate schedules by location where authoritative data is available.
Energy statisticsHistorical provider, sector and regional pricing for context and trend modeling.
Local schedulesTaxes, municipal fees and service charges tied to the property jurisdiction.
Property + weatherUse, size, climate and physical assumptions that drive expected consumption.
