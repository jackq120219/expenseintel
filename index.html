New / ExpenseIntel X-Ray

Paste anything with a price. See what it leaves out.

A lease sheet, utility bill, contractor quote, operating budget or listing can be correct and still hide the economics that matter. X-Ray detects priced lines, annualizes them, compares like-for-like layers with the location model, surfaces missing cost categories and finds escalation language worth reviewing.

01 / DETECT

What numbers are actually in the document?

Find recurring and one-time dollar lines without forcing every number into the same category.

02 / COMPARE

Which lines are far from the location model?

Recognized electricity, tax, insurance, water and other layers can be compared against the same address, use and size.

03 / MISSING

What cost did the quote never mention?

Keep likely obligations visible even when the pasted material presents itself as an all-in number.

04 / ESCALATE

Which sentence changes the economics later?

Surface percentage-based escalation, CPI and recurring fee language before it disappears into the fine print.
