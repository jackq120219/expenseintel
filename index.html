04 / Evidence discipline

Unknown is a useful answer. Made-up precision is not.

Every ExpenseIntel surface is designed to keep four evidence classes separate. That makes the product more useful as a decision system: you can see what is known, what came from a public source, what you supplied, and what remains a model.

VERIFIEDPlace → identity

Address resolution and returned geography.

PUBLICEvidence → signal

Authoritative or public data where a connector returns it.

USERPlan → facts

Use, size, service data, timeline, triggers, documents and project choices.

MODELEDAssumption → screen

Cost, demand, stress, fragility and pattern logic remain labeled as models.
