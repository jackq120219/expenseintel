Pricing / Product economics
Keep the useful part free. Charge for deeper work.

Free to check. Paid when the decision deserves depth.

ExpenseIntel should not manufacture a paywall around basic public information. The free product should be genuinely useful. Paid value begins when ExpenseIntel performs deeper diligence, preserves and monitors decisions, or handles work at professional and portfolio scale.

Future commercial tier

Do not charge because a user clicked too many times.

The free Check should stay useful. ExpenseIntel earns the right to charge when it does materially harder work: verifies a market price, decomposes a full quote, models the complete burden of a decision, watches it over time, or manages many decisions for a professional team. Complex industrial, acquisition and development diligence can eventually support custom enterprise pricing when the data coverage is deep enough.

02 / Revenue ladder

Revenue should follow increasing consequence.

The commercial model gets stronger when each paid step solves a problem the free product cannot solve completely.

03 / Why users pay

Charge when ExpenseIntel can save or protect real money.

People do not pay for another dashboard. They pay when ExpenseIntel uncovers a bad price, catches a missing scope item, quantifies a hidden obligation, identifies a better alternative, or keeps watching a decision after the first check.
