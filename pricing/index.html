Future commercial tier

Industrial and development cases should not be priced like a consumer calculator.

When ExpenseIntel eventually has utility-tariff, parcel-tax, water, incentive and infrastructure connectors deep enough for serious diligence, complex industrial and development cases can support higher one-off or enterprise pricing. 03 / Why users pay

The product is valuable when it changes the decision.

A location can create years of recurring obligations. The willingness to pay comes from identifying a bad commitment, finding a cheaper alternative, quantifying a stress case or revealing a cost variable worth negotiating before the contract is signed.
